#  CIVICLENS
### Every Promise. Real Evidence. Visible Progress.

CivicLens is an AI-powered civic accountability platform that helps citizens track public promises, explore local development projects, and examine evidence of reported progress.

Our mission is to turn civic updates from simple claims into transparent, traceable project histories.

## The Problem

How can citizens know whether a promised road repair, public facility, or neighbourhood improvement has actually progressed?

Information is often scattered, progress is difficult to track, and citizens may lack an accessible way to question misleading updates.

## Our Solution

CivicLens brings civic promises, project updates, and evidence together in one platform.

- Before & After Evidence: Document civic work with photographs.
- Location-Based Checks: Compare image metadata with device location to flag inconsistencies.
- Promise-to-Progress Tracking: Follow projects from promises to reported outcomes.
- AI-Assisted Analysis: Summarize updates and highlight claims that may need further review.
- Transparent History: Preserve caption-edit records and evidence-check results.
- Citizen Participation: Let citizens report concerns and follow review outcomes.

## What Makes CivicLens Different?

Unlike a conventional social feed, CivicLens focuses on the evidence behind civic progress.

Each project can connect a public promise, before-and-after photographs, location checks, and a traceable activity history. Citizens can see what has been reported, which technical checks have passed, and what still requires verification.

## Community Demand & Validation

A civic platform should be built around citizens real needs—not assumptions.

We aim to understand how useful CivicLens could be through community feedback and user research.

Our survey will explore:
- How many people find it difficult to track local development.
- How many would use a platform to follow public promises.
- How much citizens value location-based evidence and progress reports.
- Which features would make civic information more useful and trustworthy.

## Technology Stack

| Component | Technology |

## Expected Impact

CivicLens aims to:
- Make local development information easier to access.
- Encourage evidence-based accountability.
- Help citizens compare promises with reported progress.
- Support informed civic participation.
- Make project histories and review outcomes more transparent.

We plan to measure impact through community feedback, documented projects, evidence-check results, and citizen participation.
